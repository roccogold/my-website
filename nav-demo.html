nav ideas · demo

Each block is a live version of one idea, using the site's font and light-theme colours. Hover, scroll and click inside them. The fake switch and speaker stand in for the real controls.

01reveal on scroll up

Today the nav hides once you're 50px down and only returns at the top. Here it slides away while you scroll down, slides back on any scroll up, and the active pill already sits on the section you're in.

scroll down inside the frame, then nudge back up

02active vs hover

"Where I am" is the filled pill; "where I'm pointing" is a thin outline that glides between links. When the pointer leaves the nav, the outline springs back onto the active link and fades. Click a link to make it active.

03ahover: lift

A 2px lift and a hair of letter-spacing on the hovered label, on top of the gliding outline.

03bhover: character scramble

The hovered label briefly scrambles through glyphs and resolves left to right, in the spirit of the site's pixel/mono type.

04scroll progress + glide-then-scroll

A hairline along the pill's bottom edge fills as you read, echoing the ruler on the right of the site. Clicking a link glides the active pill there first, then scrolls, so it reads as one motion.

scroll, or click a link in the frame

05mobile: floating section pill

Phones have no nav today. A small pill at the bottom shows the section you're in; tap it to open the links. It updates as you scroll.
